Planning Risks & Assumptions
Sherpa Migration Carries Q1 Spillover Risk
Full migration completion in Q2 assumes Q1 data parity work closes on schedule. Any Q1 slip extends the Q2 window and may delay contact center integration.
Clara GA Depends on Labs Feedback Cycle
Clara GA timeline assumes Labs release ships by end of Q1 and customer feedback is captured within the first 4 weeks of Q2. Delays in Labs extend the GA date.
Stretch Goals Are Capacity-gated
21 stretch items assume no significant unplanned work in Q2. Historical unplanned rate is 15–20% of capacity. Teams should not commit to stretch items until week 4 of the quarter.
Atria Commitments Require Early Alignment
Both Atria L&B Upgrade and Atria Fabric upgrade appear in Q2. Coordination between BI and L&B teams is required to avoid scope conflicts on shared Atria touchpoints.
