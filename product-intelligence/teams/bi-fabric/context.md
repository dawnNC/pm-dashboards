# BI / Fabric — Product Context

## What It Is
Business intelligence and reporting for Aline CRM customers. Reports are built on Microsoft Fabric (migrating from Power BI / SSRS / Qlik). The Helix team owns all reporting infrastructure, data pipelines, and the Compass self-service library.

## Key Initiatives
- **Fabric migration:** Moving all CRM reporting onto Fabric-based infrastructure
- **Compass:** Self-service reporting library for DSL (Distinct Senior Living) customers
- **Sherpa reports:** Migrating legacy Sherpa CRM customers onto Aline reporting
- **Benchmark data:** 2025 benchmark reporting finalization

## Key Customers / Segments
- DSL (Distinct Senior Living): large operator, custom reporting requirements
- CRM customers migrating from standard models to Fabric

## Tech Stack Notes
- Microsoft Fabric (primary, in migration)
- Power BI (legacy, being migrated)
- SSRS / Qlik (legacy)
- dbt + Dagster in use for some pipelines

## Sprint Cadence
