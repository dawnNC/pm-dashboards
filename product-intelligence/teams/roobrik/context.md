# Roobrik — Product Context

## What It Is
Roobrik is a survey-based senior living assessment tool. Embedded on community and operator websites, it helps prospects self-identify their care needs and get matched to appropriate senior living options. Acquired by Aline; now branded "Roobrik Surveys by Aline."

## Customer Segment
Senior living operators and communities who want to convert more website visitors into qualified leads. Roobrik sits at the top of the sales funnel — it generates leads for Aline CRM customers.

## Key Integrations
- Aline CRM (lead handoff via click-through)
- HubSpot (currently — being replaced; initiative active)
- SSO via Aline (in progress)
