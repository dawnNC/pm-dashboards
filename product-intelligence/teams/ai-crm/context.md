# AI CRM — Product Context

## What It Is
AI features embedded in Aline CRM for senior living sales teams. Goal: help sales counselors triage, prioritize, and move faster with AI-assisted scoring, drafts, and next-step recommendations.

## Key Feature Areas
- **Opportunity Scoring:** AI-ranked lead list based on behavior signals (pre-tour focus)
- **AI Text Drafts:** Auto-generated follow-up messages in sales counselor voice
- **Clara:** Prospect-facing chat assistant (Labs)
- **Smart Next Step:** AI-recommended follow-up action surfaced in call summary
- **AI Pro:** Separate model tier for power users/enterprise

## Labs Release Model
New AI features ship to Labs first. Early adopter communities opt in. Feedback informs GA.

## Sprint Cadence
