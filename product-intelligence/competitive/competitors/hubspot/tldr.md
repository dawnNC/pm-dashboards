## Strengths
- Best-in-class general CRM UX and marketing automation
- Strong AI features (content generation, scoring, sequences)
- Massive ecosystem; widely known
- Free tier drives adoption

## Weaknesses
- No senior living-specific workflows or terminology
- No care/census/occupancy context
- Not designed for sales counselor workflows
- Customization required adds cost and complexity

## Why Operators Consider It
- Already using it for marketing before Aline CRM adoption
- Lower price point for small operators
- Familiarity
