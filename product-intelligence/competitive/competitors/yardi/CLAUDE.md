## Summary
Yardi is the dominant ERP in senior living. Its CRM module is broad but not deep — not purpose-built for sales counselors. Operators often use Yardi for financials and billing while using Aline for CRM. Key risk: Yardi improves its CRM capabilities to be "good enough."
